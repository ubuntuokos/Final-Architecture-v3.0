# FA3 commercial licensing boundary

## Open core

FA3-original work that is identified as Apache-2.0 may be used commercially
under the Apache License 2.0. No separate paid license is required merely
because a user or organization uses that open-core work for a commercial
purpose.

## Separately licensed modules or services

A module, service, asset pack, provider integration, support offering, hosted
service, or other deliverable may use separate commercial terms only when that
deliverable is **explicitly marked** with a different license or contract.
Nothing in this file changes the license of Apache-2.0-covered work.

Commercial entitlements are represented in FA3 by opaque entitlement
references. Secret keys, tokens, license keys, or credentials must remain in
the Secret Broker or another admitted secret provider and must not be stored in
canonical configuration or evidence.

## Third-party material

Third-party software, models, datasets, media, fonts, SDKs, codecs and services
retain their original terms. Their commercial-use or redistribution rights
must be derived from evidence and the applicable terms; they must not be
inferred from the FA3 open-core license.

## Contract boundary

This document describes repository policy. It is not a substitute for a
separate commercial agreement where one is required.
