identifiers associated with **FA3** or **Final Architecture** may be protected
under applicable trademark or unfair-competition law whether or not formally
registered.

Permitted without separate permission:

- truthful nominative references to FA3 or Final Architecture;
- statements describing compatibility, interoperability, or origin;
- reproduction required by an applicable open-source license or NOTICE.

Not granted by the software license:

- a right to imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or official status;
- a right to use FA3 branding for a modified distribution in a way that is
  likely to cause confusion as to source or approval.

This policy does not assert that any particular mark is registered. Separate
written trademark permission, where required, is outside the Apache-2.0 grant.
